Requesting Data Deletion

If you have interacted with the Guarented Rentals Instagram integration demonstration and would like your associated information deleted, you may submit a deletion request.

How to Submit a Request

  1. Send an email to [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL].
  2. Use the subject: Instagram Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include your Instagram username and, if available, the relevant comment link or comment ID.
  4. Describe the information you want deleted.

What Happens Next?

After receiving and verifying a request, the authorized team will identify the relevant information held by the demonstration application and take appropriate steps to delete it from applicable Salesforce records and application logs, subject to applicable legal obligations.

We will communicate with you if additional information is needed to process your request.

Important Information

Deleting information from this demonstration does not automatically delete the original Instagram comment. You may also need to delete the comment directly through Instagram.
