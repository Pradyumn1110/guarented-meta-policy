Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how [LEGAL BUSINESS NAME], operating the Guarented Rentals POC, handles information received through its Instagram integration demonstration.

1. Purpose of the Application

The application is a proof-of-concept integration designed to demonstrate how comments posted on the connected Guarented Rentals Instagram Business account can be processed and converted into customer service cases in Salesforce.

2. Information We Process

When a person comments on a connected Instagram post, the application may process the following information:

  • Instagram username or account identifier.
  • Comment text.
  • Comment ID.
  • Instagram media or post ID.
  • Comment timestamp and related webhook information, where provided by Meta.

We do not intentionally collect passwords, payment information, or sensitive personal information through this integration.

3. How We Use Information

Information is processed for the following purposes:

  • Identify customer service-related comments.
  • Classify comments into categories such as Repair and Refund using predefined keyword rules.
  • Identify the product mentioned in a comment, where possible.
  • Create corresponding customer service cases in Salesforce for demonstration purposes.

The current demonstration uses rule-based processing and does not require an AI service.

4. Third-Party Services

The demonstration uses the following services:

  • Meta / Instagram: To receive comment notifications and related information through Meta's APIs.
  • Salesforce: To create and manage demonstration customer service cases.
  • GitHub Pages: To host this privacy policy and data deletion information.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Comment information processed by the demonstration may be included in Salesforce case records. Temporary processing information may also appear in application logs during testing.

Information is retained only for the period necessary for the demonstration and its operational requirements. Demo records and logs should be deleted when no longer required, subject to applicable legal obligations.

6. Data Sharing

Information is not intended to be sold. It is shared with the service providers described above only as needed to operate and demonstrate the integration. Access should be restricted to authorized personnel.

7. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information processed during the demonstration. Access to credentials and application systems should be restricted to authorized personnel.

8. Your Rights and Data Requests

You may request information about, access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information processed through this demonstration, subject to applicable law.

Please refer to our Data Deletion Instructions for details on submitting a request.
